# Code of Conduct

Simple Organizer is a small project, and everyone who tests, reports, asks or contributes is welcome here –
no matter their experience, background, identity or how good their English is.

## Our standards

Please:

- be kind, patient and respectful, especially to beginners,
- give and accept helpful, constructive feedback,
- assume good intentions and focus on what helps the project and its users.

Not okay:

- insults, personal attacks, harassment or trolling,
- sexualised language or imagery, discriminatory jokes or remarks,
- publishing other people's private information,
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a friendly, professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently block people from the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, please report it privately:

- use **Report content** in the "…" menu of the comment or issue on GitHub, or
- report the user to GitHub via their profile → **Block or report**.

Reports are treated confidentially.
